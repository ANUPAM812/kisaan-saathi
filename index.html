Forecast kahan se aata hai?

Hawa, nami aur tapman ke niyam Navier-Stokes aur thermodynamics ke samikaran hain. Supercomputer par ECMWF, NOAA GFS, DWD ICON jaise Numerical Weather Prediction models inhe puri dharti ke grid par hal karte hain. Open-Meteo in sabka data ek API me deta hai.

Bhari baarish ki chetavni IMD ke maapdand par hai: 64.5 mm/din se upar "heavy" maana jata hai.

🧪 Chemical engineering

Mitti ek chemical reactor hai

Ion exchange: clay ke kan negative charge wale hote hain aur K⁺, Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺, NH₄⁺ ko pakad kar rakhte hain. Yahi CEC hai.

pH equilibrium: pH badalte hi phosphate (H₂PO₄⁻ ↔ HPO₄²⁻) aur Al³⁺, Fe²⁺ ki ghulansheelta badalti hai. pH 6-7.5 me poshan sabse zyada milta hai.

Mineralogy: montmorillonite paani se phoolti hai (kaali mitti ki dararen), kaolinite nahi. Fe₂O₃ (hematite) mitti ko laal karta hai.

Data: ISRIC SoilGrids machine learning se 250 m ke grid par clay, sand, pH, carbon batata hai.

🏗️ Civil engineering

Paani ka hisaab (Hydrology)

Khet ek reservoir hai. Mass conservation: jo paani aaya − jo gaya = jo mitti me bacha.

ΔS = Baarish + Sinchai − ET − Runoff − Drainage

Darcy ka niyam: q = −K · dh/dz. Clay me K chhota (dheema nikasi), sand me bada (tez nikasi).

Soil-water tank: Field capacity aur Wilting point ke beech ka paani hi fasal use karti hai. Aadha khali hote hi (MAD 50%) sinchai ki salah di jati hai.

🔁 App me yeh sab step-by-step

  1. Location → coordinates (Geocoding API ya GPS)
  2. Live data: 30 din purana + 16 din agla mausam (Open-Meteo) aur us jagah ki mitti (SoilGrids)
  3. Mitti ka model: 4 jawab + SoilGrids → clay/silt/sand, mitti ka type, paani rokne ki kshamta
  4. Water-balance simulation: har din ET₀ (FAO-56) × fasal ka Kc, minus baarish, tanki me kitna paani bacha
  5. Risk engine: jalbharav, sookha aur garmi-thand ke din gin kar 0-100 score
  6. Aasaan bhasha: sab kuch salah me badal diya jata hai
